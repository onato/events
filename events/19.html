🎶 Drum Tao

Fri 19 Mar 2027, 7:30pm · Theatre Royal, Nelson 🌏 international

When: Fri 19 Mar 2027, 7:30pm
Where: Theatre Royal, Nelson

Japanese taiko troupe Drum Tao brings its high-energy show of drumming, martial-arts athleticism and cinematic staging to Nelson. The group has played to over 10 million people worldwide.

Drum Tao is a Japanese taiko ensemble based at TAO no Oka in Aso Kuju National Park. Its shows combine massive drums, choreography and martial-arts-style athleticism with lighting and staging that reviewers have likened to a ritual. The Japan Times and CBC Arts have praised the troupe (as quoted on a venue page). This is a rare touring international spectacle for the region.

Why you might like it: Rare international percussion theatre reaching Nelson, in the same spirit as the Machine de Cirque night you loved.
Tickets: $59.90–$89.90, Theatre Royal box office
