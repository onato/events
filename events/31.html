🎬 Real Talk: Day One short film screening new

Tue 1 Dec 2026, 7:00pm · WAA! Club, Nelson

When: Tue 1 Dec 2026, 7:00pm
Where: WAA! Club, Nelson

A 65-minute programme of eight short NZ films from Day One's emerging filmmakers, tackling Alzheimer's, harassment in the music scene, colonisation and cultural restoration, toxic masculinity and more. Intimate session for around 20 people.

Day One is a New Zealand filmmaker-development organisation whose shorts premiere at festivals such as Show Me Shorts. This Real Talk curation of eight films mixes social commentary with a sci-fi space adventure and a film on the cost of art without soul. The Nelson session at WAA! Club is hosted by Jo and Alan and opens a festival week; the same lineup screens in Auckland on 29 Nov.

Why you might like it: A small-room evening of fresh NZ short films with something to say, right in your short-film lane.
