🪔 Afrofest 2026 Nelson new

Sat 21 Nov 2026, 2:00pm–5:30pm · Saxton Netball Pavilion, Nelson

When: Sat 21 Nov 2026, 2:00pm–5:30pm
Where: Saxton Netball Pavilion, Nelson

An African cultural festival put on by Nelson's own African community, with live music, cultural performances and food.

Afrofest Nelson is a community-run celebration of African cultures in Nelson, with live music, performances and food across an afternoon at Saxton. The listing gives no set programme or ticket price, so it is worth checking the organisers' page closer to the day.

Why you might like it: A community-made window onto African music and food, in the same spirit as Diwali and the Multicultural Festival you've enjoyed.
