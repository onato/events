🖼️ Cornelia Parker new

10 Oct 2026 – 7 Mar 2027 · City Gallery Wellington Te Whare Toi, Wellington 🌏 international 🧭 worth a trip

When: 10 Oct 2026 – 7 Mar 2027
Where: City Gallery Wellington Te Whare Toi, Wellington

The first public survey in New Zealand of British conceptual artist Cornelia Parker, with over fifty works including Thirty Pieces of Silver, Cold Dark Matter and War Room. It follows her 2022 Tate Britain retrospective and marks the reopening of City Gallery Wellington.

Cornelia Parker is known for exploding, charring, crushing and reconfiguring everyday objects, so that familiar things are made to carry questions about violence, memory and value. This show is curated by Andrea Schlieker and Aaron Lister and is the gallery's first major exhibition since it reopened in Te Ngākau Civic Square. The wider claims about its reception come from the gallery and its promoters, and I found no independent critical review of this particular showing.

Why you might like it: A conceptual artist whose work is built around a real idea, and an international survey you won't get closer than Wellington, with time to plan a trip before it closes on 7 March.
