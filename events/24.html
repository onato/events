🎶 Twisted Frequency Festival 2027 new

Wed 30 Dec 2026 · Cobb Valley, Golden Bay 🌏 international

When: Wed 30 Dec 2026
Where: Cobb Valley, Golden Bay

Days of Aotearoa underground electronic music and culture in the Cobb Valley, across four stages with workshops and performers. R18.

Twisted Frequency is a multi-day bush festival of New Zealand underground music and culture, with hundreds of artists over four stages plus workshops. Organisers say its longer-term future is uncertain, which makes this edition feel like a now-or-maybe-never one. General admission is listed at $500; no lineup was published on the listing I saw.

Why you might like it: Experimental and electronic music in a remote valley, and it may be one of the last editions.
Tickets: $500 general admission (R18)
