🎬 NZ Independent Film Festival 2026

19 Nov – 21 Nov 2026, 6:00pm · State Cinema, Nelson 🌏 international

When: 19 Nov – 21 Nov 2026, 6:00pm
Where: State Cinema, Nelson

Three evenings of international short films plus a New Zealand feature and a Mexican feature, in its 4th year. Genres range from arthouse and experimental to animation and sci-fi.

The NZ Independent Film Festival screens short and feature films from around the world, many of them award winners at other festivals. This year's programme includes a New Zealand feature, a Mexican feature and a range of international shorts across drama, arthouse, experimental, animation and music video. The organisers say one film that premiered at the festival went on to qualify for the Oscars. Extra screenings at the WAA! Club are booked separately.

Why you might like it: A small-town chance to see international shorts and arthouse features on a big screen, which is your kind of evening.
Tickets: $18 general admission
