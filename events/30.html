🎻 Celtic Connections with Carlos Núñez (NZSO) new

Fri 21 May 2027, 6:30pm · Wellington Town Hall, Wellington 🌏 international 🧭 worth a trip

When: Fri 21 May 2027, 6:30pm
Where: Wellington Town Hall, Wellington

Galician piper and flautist Carlos Núñez makes his NZ debut with the NZSO under James Judd, in a programme of Mendelssohn's Scottish Symphony, Beethoven's Irish, Scottish and Welsh folk-song arrangements and Celtic surprises. Also plays Christchurch (23 May), Oamaru and Dunedin; Nelson is not on the tour.

Carlos Núñez is one of the best-known gaita (Galician bagpipe) players in the world, a Vigo-born multi-instrumentalist who has spent three decades bridging Celtic folk traditions and classical and world music, collaborating widely across genres. This is one of his NZSO debut appearances in the orchestra's 80th-anniversary 2027 season, in the reopened Wellington Town Hall. The programme pairs Mendelssohn's Scottish Symphony with Beethoven's arrangements of Irish, Scottish and Welsh melodies, with the piper and flute soloist woven through.

Why you might like it: A genuinely rare visiting folk virtuoso playing with a full orchestra, and May is far enough away to plan a Wellington trip around it.
Tickets: NZSO member and casual tickets via nzso.co.nz
