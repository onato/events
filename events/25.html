🪔 Ven. Robina Courtin: Be Your Own Therapist (and two further teachings) new

17 Nov – 19 Nov 2026, 6:30pm · Chandrakirti Community Events Centre, Upper Moutere 🌏 international 🃏 wildcard

When: 17 Nov – 19 Nov 2026, 6:30pm
Where: Chandrakirti Community Events Centre, Upper Moutere

Venerable Robina Courtin, the Australian-born Tibetan Buddhist nun known for her prison work and teaching, gives a public talk on 17 Nov, an open teaching on 18 Nov and a one-day immersion on 19 Nov at Chandrakirti.

Three events at Chandrakirti Meditation Centre: 'Be Your Own Therapist' (public talk, Tue 17 Nov, 6:30-8pm, suggested donation $20); 'The Natural Karma: We Create Our Own Reality' (Wed 18 Nov, 6:30-8pm, $20 donation); and 'A Bird Needs Two Wings: Wisdom and Compassion', a one-day immersion on Thu 19 Nov, 9:30am-5pm ($80 including lunch), followed by a Guru Puja at 6pm. Sign-up is through the form linked on the event page.

Why you might like it: A rare chance to hear a remarkable, plain-speaking thinker on how people see and change their own minds, in a short window this week.
Tickets: $20 donation per talk; $80 for the Thursday day immersion
