🎻 Resurrection – Mahler Symphony No. 2 (NZSO, Town Hall reopening) new

25 Feb – 26 Feb 2027, 6:30pm · Te Whare Whakarauika Wellington Town Hall, Wellington 🧭 worth a trip

When: 25 Feb – 26 Feb 2027, 6:30pm
Where: Te Whare Whakarauika Wellington Town Hall, Wellington

The NZSO under Gemma New marks the reopening of the renovated Wellington Town Hall with Mahler's Second Symphony, with soprano Madeleine Pierard, mezzo Deborah Humble and the Orpheus Choir of Wellington. A new commission from Salina Fisher opens the programme.

The NZSO is returning to its renovated home, Te Whare Whakarauika Wellington Town Hall, and has chosen Mahler's 'Resurrection' Symphony for the occasion, a work it has not played in that hall since 1968. Gemma New conducts a 100-strong orchestra and choir with soloists Madeleine Pierard and Deborah Humble. The concert opens with a world premiere by Wellington composer Salina Fisher, written for the reopening.

Why you might like it: A once-in-a-generation Mahler 2 in a freshly reopened hall, about three and a half months away, which is enough time to plan a Wellington trip.
