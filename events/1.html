🎻 Baroque Delights – NZSO Summer Tour new

Sat 5 Dec 2026, 7:30pm · Nelson Centre of Musical Arts, Nelson

When: Sat 5 Dec 2026, 7:30pm
Where: Nelson Centre of Musical Arts, Nelson

NZSO players led by Concertmaster Vesa-Matti Leppänen with soprano Anna Leese, in Pergolesi (La serva padrona, Adriano in Siria, L'Olimpiade) and a suite from Rameau's Platée. A rare chance to hear the national orchestra in Nelson.

Why you might like it: The NZSO in a small room with a top soprano is exactly the quality-first orchestral night you asked for.
Tickets: $54 adult, $50 concession
