🎻 Beethoven Piano Concertos 2, 3 & 4 with Javier Perianes

25 Sep – 30 Sep 2027, 7:30pm · Wellington (25 & 26 Sep) / Christchurch (29 & 30 Sep), Wellington / Christchurch 🌏 international 🧭 worth a trip

When: 25 Sep – 30 Sep 2027, 7:30pm
Where: Wellington (25 & 26 Sep) / Christchurch (29 & 30 Sep), Wellington / Christchurch

Spanish pianist Javier Perianes plays all five Beethoven piano concertos with the NZSO, directing from the keyboard, across two programmes. It is part of the NZSO's 2027 season, which marks 200 years since Beethoven's death.

The NZSO's 2027 season pairs the orchestra's 80th anniversary with the bicentenary of Beethoven's death. Perianes returns to play the full concerto cycle. Concertos 2, 3 and 4 are listed for Wellington on Saturday 25 September and Christchurch on Wednesday 29 September. Concertos 1 and 5 are listed for Wellington on Sunday 26 September (6:00pm) and Christchurch on Thursday 30 September.
