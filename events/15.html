🎻 Anna Lapwood: Cosmology (NZSO, with Max Richter) new

Thu 12 Aug 2027, 6:30pm · Wellington Town Hall, Wellington 🌏 international 🧭 worth a trip

When: Thu 12 Aug 2027, 6:30pm
Where: Wellington Town Hall, Wellington

English organist Anna Lapwood makes her New Zealand debut with the NZSO under André de Ridder, playing Max Richter's Cosmology, written for her, alongside Bruckner's Symphony No. 8. She also gives a solo organ recital at the Town Hall the night before.

Anna Lapwood is one of the best-known organists of her generation. Max Richter wrote Cosmology for her, and it premiered at the Royal Albert Hall in May 2025 with the Philharmonia under André de Ridder. De Ridder is the NZSO's incoming music director and conducts this Wellington performance. The NZSO lists the programme as Richter's Cosmology with Bruckner's Eighth. A separate solo organ recital is listed for Wednesday 11 August at 6:30pm. Reviews of her Richter performances in Sydney and the UK are linked below. The NZSO pages show weekdays only, so the year 2027 is inferred from the calendar and the 2027 season listing.

Why you might like it: A rare international organ-and-orchestra event that is worth a trip to Wellington, with nine months' notice to plan it.
