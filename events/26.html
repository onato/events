🖼️ The Japanese Garden in the West: 100 Years of Eastern Influence new

Wed 18 Nov 2026, 6:30pm · NCMA, Pastorius Waller Recital Theatre, Nelson 🌏 international 🃏 wildcard

When: Wed 18 Nov 2026, 6:30pm
Where: NCMA, Pastorius Waller Recital Theatre, Nelson

Amanda Herries, author of Japanese Gardens in Britain and former Museum of London curator, lectures on how Japanese gardens were created in Europe and whether Western gardens can ever be truly Japanese.

A Decorative and Fine Arts Society NZ lecture at the NCMA, 6:30-7:30pm. Herries asks whether gardens made in the West can ever be genuinely Japanese given the differences between the two traditions. Members free; guest admission $35 cash at the door (no EFTPOS).

Why you might like it: A visiting expert on a question about cross-cultural ideas, which suits your taste for art with a real idea behind it.
